What the window is for

Understand one question well enough to rule on it. Ruling is the goal; asking is how you get there; everything else is evidence.

1 · ALWAYS ON SCREEN
pinned top and bottom; never scrolls, never covered
Who is asking (project, kind, when)
The question, in plain words
Every choice as a button that explains itself (what it does, the agent's word, its key)
A way to ask (a line; at minimum size, the / key)
2 · PRIMARY READING
top of the scrolling body
Brief (what happened, what is at stake)
3 · ON DEMAND
grows below the brief once you ask
Your conversation with the explainer
4 · REFERENCE
quiet footer when there is room; folds into an IDs menu when there is not
Identifiers: plan, request, assignment, work item (a pinned footer line, click to copy; in small windows, an IDs ▾ menu in the header)
The original request verbatim (collapsed in the body)
SHRINKING
drop, never squeeze
Wide: choices are a column beside the reading, with full explanations. Narrow: they sit inline after the brief and dock as a compact strip when scrolled away. Tiers leave bottom up (4, 3, 2); tier 1 stays, only the question clamps.
TYPE ROLES
the face says who is talking
Serif: the question, and your own words
Sans: the explanation and the interface
Mono: verbatim system text, keys, IDs
